Summary

“Charade” (1963) is a stylish blend of mystery, romance, and comedy starring Audrey Hepburn and Cary Grant. Set in Paris, the film follows Regina Lampert as she’s pursued by several men who want a fortune her late husband may have stolen. With dazzling performances and a twist-filled plot, “Charade” is often called “the best Hitchcock movie Hitchcock never made.”

Cast

  • Audrey Hepburn as Regina Lampert
  • Cary Grant as Peter Joshua
  • Walter Matthau as Hamilton Bartholomew
  • James Coburn as Tex Panthollow
  • George Kennedy as Herman Scobie

Why Watch?

  • Iconic performances from Hollywood legends
  • Witty dialogue and suspenseful storytelling
  • Gorgeous Parisian backdrop
  • A score by Henry Mancini
