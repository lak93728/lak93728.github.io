“Aurora Rising”

Jean-Paul Fournier (French, b. 1969)

Bronze, 2005 Commissioned specifically for the Aurora Art Museum’s opening of its downtown wing, this abstract bronze sculpture greets visitors at the main entrance plaza. Its upward-sweeping form symbolizes growth, renewal, and the city’s evolving cultural identity.
