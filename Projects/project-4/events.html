Events

The museum is more than a gallery—it’s a gathering place. Through educational programs, artist talks, family workshops, and community partnerships, we strive to make art accessible to all. Whether you’re an art student, a curious traveler, or a family looking for inspiration, the Aurora Art Museum has something for you.

First Fridays

Join us for extended hours and special programming every first Friday evening.

Artist Talks

Meet the creators behind the work, every second Saturday.

Family Sundays

Interactive workshops designed for children and parents alike.

Film Nights

Screenings of art documentaries and experimental films.

Education

We believe art education inspires lifelong curiosity.

For schools

Experience guided field trips with hands-on activities as well as teacher resources and lesson plans .

For adults

Join our studio workshops with practicing artists and lecture series exploring art history and current trends

For families

Drop-in on art making in the Creativity Lab and seasonal family festivals
